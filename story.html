03 · Read

Read the story.

Read each sentence in Chinese. Look at the pinyin when you need help, then listen to the sentence.

01
早上好！
Zǎoshang hǎo!
Good morning!
02
小明起床了。
Xiǎomíng qǐchuáng le.
Xiaoming gets up.
03
他吃早饭。
Tā chī zǎofàn.
He eats breakfast.
04
然后，他去学校。
Ránhòu, tā qù xuéxiào.
Then, he goes to school.
05
他很开心。
Tā hěn kāixīn.
He is very happy.
